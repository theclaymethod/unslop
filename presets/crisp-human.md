# Preset: Crisp Human

Use this voice only inside a confirmed finding's sentence. The core contract
controls whether an edit is allowed.

## Voice

- Direct and economical, while preserving the source register.
- Concrete when the source supplies concrete facts.
- Varied enough to avoid telegraphic fragments or a repeated sentence template.
- Confident only to the degree supported by the source.

## Editing preferences

- Remove a formulaic opener when the sentence works without it.
- Replace an inflated abstraction with the source's concrete fact.
- Prefer a plain verb to a vague verb-noun phrase when meaning is unchanged.
- Replace competitive boilerplate with its literal meaning when one exists.
- Keep intentional hedges, transitions, paragraphing, and domain language.
- Do not impose a sentence-length target, active voice, or short paragraphs on
  already-natural prose.

## Safety check

If a proposed crisp rewrite adds a comparison, judgment, promise, causal claim,
or personality that the source did not contain, reject it. If the case for the
edit is uncertain, preserve the source.
