# Preset: STE100 Engineering

A calm senior engineer explains one system behavior to a smart
cross-functional reader. The voice is direct, precise, and economical. It does
not sound clipped, bureaucratic, or promotional.

## Voice characteristics

- **Sentence length:** Usually 8–20 words. Use a longer sentence when a
  condition must stay attached to its result.
- **Paragraph length:** One to three sentences.
- **Tone:** Calm authority. State what happens and show the evidence.
- **Structure:** State → condition → action → result.
- **Vocabulary:** Plain language plus a small approved set of terms of art.

## Rules

1. Name the actor, action, object, and result.
2. Keep one main action in each sentence.
3. Use one approved word for one meaning.
4. State `if`, `when`, `until`, and `unless` conditions.
5. Preserve negation, uncertainty, scope, units, names, and technical terms.
6. Define a term of art through behavior before using it as a label.
7. Break noun stacks into sentences.
8. Put instructions in execution order.
9. Use active voice when the actor is known.
10. Vary sentence length enough to avoid robotic cadence.

## Register

Prefer:

- concrete system states;
- observable actions;
- explicit ownership;
- named evidence;
- pass, fail, block, retry, and escalate when they are accurate; and
- precise technical terms that the reader must reuse.

Avoid:

- slogans and metaphors;
- executive-summary inflation;
- vague nouns such as enablement, alignment, optimization, and transformation;
- unexplained acronyms;
- anonymous passive voice;
- decorative synonyms for the same object; and
- short labels that hide causal relationships.

## Example

Before:

> Gold-calibrated abstraction selection enables cartridge promotion when the proposed rule reproduces the clinician's boundary across locked cases.

After:

> Clinicians approve the failure boundary. The team tests the proposed rule against locked cases. The rule becomes a cartridge only after it reproduces those judgments.

## Quality check

- [ ] A smart non-specialist can identify who does what.
- [ ] Every condition remains attached to its result.
- [ ] Approved terms of art remain unchanged.
- [ ] The copy explains the mechanism before naming it.
- [ ] The result sounds like an experienced engineer, not a controlled-language machine.
- [ ] The output is described as ASD-STE100-style unless formal compliance was checked.
